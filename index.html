03 · What went wrong on Meta

The September collapse wasn't bad luck — it was account structure

We reviewed the full September Meta Ads export. The money didn't buy results because most of it never reached the right people.

What the account looks like

  • Only 1 ad set was actually delivering. Of 13 ad sets, the rest were not delivering, error, archived or inactive — yet budget was fragmented across them.
  • It's almost entirely retargeting. Nearly every ad set targets “Website Visitors.” That pool is tiny and finite — you can't grow a spa by only re-showing ads to the same few thousand people.
  • Ad fatigue. Frequency hit 7.8–8.4× on some sets — the same people saw the same ad up to 8 times. Response collapses.
  • Duplicate “Copy” ad sets splitting budget so none ever completes Meta's learning phase.

The one bright spot — and the real issue

The single active ad set generated 2,621 WhatsApp conversations for AED 8,085 (~AED 3 each) — genuinely cheap leads.

So the problem isn't lead cost; it's that those conversations aren't turning into booked, paid appointments (hence AED 850 return). Meta is producing chat volume that doesn't convert — a creative, offer, and follow-up problem, not a reach problem.

Bottom line: Meta needs a rebuild — fresh prospecting campaigns, new creative, consolidated budget, and a booking-focused offer — not more spend on the current setup.

04 · What's working on Google

Google is doing the heavy lifting — let's pour fuel on it

September: AED 39,175 spent · 21,118 clicks · 4.05% CTR · AED 1.86 avg CPC · 98% mobile.

Your highest-intent, best-converting pockets

  • Branded search wins big: “velvet touch spa” / “velvet spa dubai” convert at 7%+ with 29–35% CTR — people searching your name are ready to book.
  • JBR location intent converts at 6.3% — your home turf is gold.
  • “Near me” demand is huge: “spa near me” (595 clicks) & “massage near me” (518) are your top traffic drivers.
  • Arabic search performs best of all: “مساج في دبي” 17.7% CTR, “مساج دبي” 14% — matching your Arabic-speaking audience.

Where Google budget is leaking

  • Performance Max ate AED 6,089 for 15,893 clicks at AED 0.38 — suspiciously cheap, 0% top-of-page: likely low-quality display/network traffic, not booking-ready searchers. Needs audit & exclusions.
  • No booking/revenue tracking in GA4 — “conversions” are WhatsApp clicks (1,949 in Sep), not confirmed paid bookings. You're optimising to clicks, not revenue.
  • Budget is spread across Nearby + Dubai-Wide + PMax — the top-converting branded/JBR/Arabic terms deserve a bigger, dedicated share.

07 · The strategy

What we'll do about it

  1. Stop the Meta bleed, then rebuild it. Pause the broken retargeting sprawl immediately. Rebuild with 1–2 clean prospecting campaigns targeting new nearby clients (JBR/Marina/Jumeirah), fresh aromatherapy-led creative, consolidated budget, and a booking offer — not just “start a chat.”
  2. Reallocate & scale Google. Shift budget toward the proven winners — branded, JBR, “near me” and Arabic search — and give PMax a proper audit (exclusions, asset refresh) or cap it until it earns.
  3. Install real revenue tracking. Set up proper booking/lead-value tracking (GA4 + WhatsApp + offline booking match) so every dirham is optimised to actual bookings, not just clicks. This single fix changes how every future campaign is judged.
  4. Double down on Arabic & aromatherapy. Dedicated Arabic ad copy + landing section, and aromatherapy as the hero offer across ads, remarketing and social — it's what sells.
  5. Turn social & YouTube into bookings. A consistent Instagram (@spavelvetjbr) + short-video content engine that feeds the funnel and captures the 2,000+ monthly YouTube visitors you already attract.
  6. Report on what matters. Monthly ROAS-by-channel and cost-per-booking reporting (like this), so decisions are made on profit, not vanity metrics.
